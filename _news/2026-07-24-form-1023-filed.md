**ESB AI Lab submits Form 1023 application for federal 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status.** The application, prepared by Foundation Group under the SureStart Complete package, was filed with the Internal Revenue Service seeking recognition as a tax-exempt public charity under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. Upon favorable determination, contributions to ESB AI Lab are expected to be tax-deductible retroactive to the date of incorporation, April 22, 2026. The organization was incorporated as a California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation for scientific, educational, and charitable purposes. Processing times for Form 1023 applications typically range from three to six months.
