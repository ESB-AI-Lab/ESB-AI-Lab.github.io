**ESB AI Lab releases HapSolo2, a GPU-accelerated tool for removing secondary haplotigs from diploid genome assemblies.** HapSolo2 was developed with lab alumni Juan Yin, Mansi Agrawal, and Prashansa Goel from Dr. Solares' research at UC Davis and UC San Diego, and the manuscript was completed at ESB AI Lab. HapSolo2 reproduces the published results of the original HapSolo while adding GPU acceleration, new optimizers, and up to 7.6 times faster run times, with a wrapper that simplifies the pipeline into commands such as align, search, train, and classify. Across four diploid assemblies, HapSolo2 reduced duplicated orthologs from 52.0% to 5.6% in avocado, with ortholog completeness decreasing by at most 1.3 percentage points. The preprint is available on <a href="https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.02.756147v1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">bioRxiv</a>, and the software is available on <a href="https://github.com/ESB-AI-Lab/HapSolo/releases/tag/2.0" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GitHub</a>.
