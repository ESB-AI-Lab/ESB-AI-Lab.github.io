**ESB AI Lab presents at the inaugural Resilient California Summit.** ESB AI Lab, a California nonprofit research organization, is attending the <a href="https://resilientcaliforniasummit.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Resilient California Summit</a> in Sacramento to present Dr. Edwin Solares' work on using AI for species conservation and food security. The poster presentation outlines how the lab's computer vision work is deployed to edge AI devices for real-time use in the field. Dr. Solares is presenting work previously funded under the CA CARES program while he was University of California faculty in San Diego, in which zero net energy devices were developed to help researchers, government agencies, and tribal nations monitor microclimates, species diversity and invasive species and make decisions that improve land stewardship.
